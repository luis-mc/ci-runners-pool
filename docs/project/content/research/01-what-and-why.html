How to read it. How it was checked: Checked (docs) means confirmed in the other tools' own published docs or in GitHub's docs; Checked (code) means confirmed in crsp's own specs and code; Reported means seen once and not confirmed. Value: High, Medium or Low for a pool of your own PCs that exists to save you money. Status uses the same words as Full Progress: To do, Running, Waiting, Needs you, Built, Live.
